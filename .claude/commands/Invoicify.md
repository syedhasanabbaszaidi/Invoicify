### 1. BUSINESS DOMAIN & CORE WORKFLOW
We manage an office/collective of independent software developers and QA testers in India. Each team member operates under their own independent Indian GST sole-proprietorship / freelance entity and bills international clients (e.g., in South Africa, USA, UK) in **USD** as zero-rated exports under **LUT (Letter of Undertaking)** without payment of IGST.

**Key Operational Rules:**
1. **Admin-Centric Multi-Entity Management:** An Admin user logs in and generates monthly invoices on behalf of multiple freelancers. (Also support role-based access where individual freelancers can optionally log in to view only their own invoices/bank profile, while Admins can manage everything).
2. **Flexible Monthly Billing:** Not every freelancer bills every month, and a freelancer might bill one or more client companies in a given month (though typically one primary client per month).
3. **Batch End-of-Month Billing Wizard (Critical Flow):** On any day (e.g., last week of the month), the Admin selects:
   - Target Billing Month & Year (e.g., October 2026) and Invoice Date
   - Target Customer Company (Billed To)
   - One or more Freelancers from the roster
   - For each selected freelancer: selects/customizes their work description line items (from a reusable Service Description Master) and enters the **Total USD Amount** for that month.
   - The system auto-generates unique, sequential, freelancer-specific invoice numbers (e.g., `RS/SA/10/2026` following each freelancer's prefix pattern) and generates a **2-Page PDF Invoice** per freelancer.
4. **2-Page PDF Output Specification:**
   - **Page 1:** Official Indian GST Export Tax Invoice (matching exact statutory fields: Supplier Name, Address, GSTIN, LUT declaration, Invoice Serial No., Date, Place of Supply: Outside India, Country of Supply, Receiver Name/Address/State/Country, Line Items table with S.No, Description of Services, SAC Code, Total, Taxable Value, CGST/SGST/IGST at 0.00%, Invoice Total in USD, Reverse Charge declaration, Certification clause, Jurisdiction clause, and Authorized Signatory block/signature image).
   - **Page 2:** Dedicated **Wire Transfer / Telegraphic Transfer Payment Instructions (USD)** specific to that freelancer—rendered either from structured freelancer bank fields into a clean formatted sheet OR by appending/embedding the freelancer's uploaded bank instruction document/image.
5. **Consolidated & Individual Dashboard + USD-to-INR Reconciliation:**
   - View consolidated monthly billing across all freelancers (total USD billed, total INR realized, pending USD) as well as individual freelancer ledgers.
   - **Inward Remittance Reconciliation:** When international wire transfers arrive in INR, the Admin records the settlement against an invoice: Actual USD Received (accounting for any intermediary bank wire fees), Applied Bank Exchange Rate (USD -> INR), Net INR Credited, FIRC / Bank Reference Number, and Settlement Date.

### 2. TECH STACK (VERCEL + NEON OPTIMIZED)
- **Framework:** Next.js 15+ (App Router), TypeScript (strict mode)
- **Database:** Neon Serverless PostgreSQL (`@neondatabase/serverless`) + **Drizzle ORM** (with `drizzle-kit` migrations and seed script)
- **Authentication:** NextAuth.js v5 (Auth.js) with Credentials (email/password hashed via `bcryptjs`) + role-based middleware (`ADMIN`, `FREELANCER`)
- **UI & Styling:** Tailwind CSS + `shadcn/ui` + Lucide Icons + TanStack Table (for sortable/filterable invoice & reconciliation ledgers)
- **PDF Generation (Vercel Serverless Safe):** Use `@react-pdf/renderer` (pure JS/React PDF generation that runs reliably on Vercel Serverless Functions without Puppeteer/Chromium binary size limits) AND `pdf-lib` (so if a freelancer uploads an existing PDF for their Page 2 Bank Details, we can seamlessly merge Page 1 generated by `@react-pdf/renderer` with their uploaded Page 2 PDF, or render structured bank details on Page 2 if no raw PDF is uploaded).
- **File Storage:** Vercel Blob (`@vercel/blob`) (with a clean local/base64 fallback adapter if `BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN` is absent in local dev) for storing freelancer signature images and Page 2 bank detail PDFs/images.
- **Validation:** Zod + React Hook Form

### 3. DATABASE SCHEMA (DRIZZLE ORM / POSTGRESQL)
Design normalized tables with proper foreign keys, indexes, and timestamps:

1. **`users`**:
   - `id`, `name`, `email`, `passwordHash`, `role` (`ADMIN` | `FREELANCER`), `freelancerId` (nullable FK), `isActive`, `createdAt`
2. **`freelancers`**:
   - `id`, `fullName`, `tradeName` (optional), `email`, `phone`, `addressLine1`, `addressLine2`, `city`, `state`, `pincode`, `gstin` (e.g., `09BRLPS5237E1ZF`), `pan`, `lutNumber`, `lutFinancialYear` (e.g., `2026-27`), `invoicePrefix` (e.g., `RS/SA`), `nextSequenceNumber` (integer), `defaultSacCode` (e.g., `998313`), `jurisdictionCity` (e.g., `Gautam Buddh Nagar (Noida)`), `signatureImageUrl` (nullable), `isActive`, `createdAt`, `updatedAt`
3. **`freelancer_bank_profiles`** (1-to-1 or versioned active profile per freelancer for Page 2):
   - `id`, `freelancerId` (FK),
   - **Intermediary / Correspondent / Nostro Bank:** `intermediaryBankName` (e.g., `J.P. Morgan Chase Bank N.A., New York`), `intermediarySwift` (e.g., `CHASUS33`), `chipsUid` (e.g., `0002`), `fedAbaRouting` (e.g., `0210-0002-1`), `nostroAccountNumber` (e.g., `11407376 (Nostro Account of Axis Bank, Mumbai - SWIFT: AXISINBB)`),
   - **Beneficiary Bank (India):** `beneficiaryBankName` (e.g., `Axis Bank Ltd.`), `beneficiaryBranchAddress` (e.g., `Sector-62, Noida`), `beneficiarySwift` (e.g., `AXISINBB723`), `beneficiaryIfsc`,
   - **Final Beneficiary:** `accountHolderName`, `accountNumber`, `accountHolderAddress`,
   - **Purpose Code:** `purposeCode` (e.g., `P0802`), `purposeDescription` (e.g., `Software implementation/consultancy (other than those covered in SOFTEX form)`),
   - **Uploaded Override Option:** `uploadedBankDocUrl` (nullable PDF/PNG URL if the freelancer wants their exact bank-issued PDF attached as Page 2 instead of the generated template), `useUploadedDocForPage2` (boolean, default `false`), `updatedAt`
4. **`customers`** (Client Companies):
   - `id`, `companyName` (e.g., `Kwantu Information Technology`), `addressLine1`, `addressLine2`, `city`, `stateProvince` (e.g., `Western Cape`), `postalCode` (e.g., `7700`), `country` (e.g., `South Africa`), `stateCode` (nullable), `taxIdOrGstin` (nullable), `billingEmail`, `defaultCurrency` (default `USD`), `paymentTermsNotes`, `isActive`, `createdAt`
5. **`service_item_masters`** (Reusable Work Description Templates):
   - `id`, `title` (short label), `descriptionLine` (e.g., `Manual testing consultancy`, `Test cases review and consultancy SENDP`, `SENDP React configuration`), `defaultSacCode` (e.g., `998313`), `category` (`QA` | `DEV` | `DEVOPS` | `CONSULTING`), `isActive`
6. **`invoices`**:
   - `id`, `invoiceNumber` (unique per freelancer, e.g., `RS/SA/10/2026`), `freelancerId` (FK), `customerId` (FK), `billingMonth` (1-12), `billingYear` (e.g., 2026), `invoiceDate` (date),
   - `placeOfSupply` (default `Outside India`), `countryOfSupply` (copied from customer country),
   - `currency` (default `USD`), `subtotalUsd` (numeric 12,2), `cgstRate` (0), `cgstAmount` (0), `sgstRate` (0), `sgstAmount` (0), `igstRate` (0), `igstAmount` (0), `totalUsd` (numeric 12,2),
   - `lutDeclarationText` (snapshot of LUT declaration at time of generation),
   - `bankSnapshotJson` (JSONB snapshot of freelancer bank details at time of generation so historical invoices never mutate if bank details change later),
   - `status` (`DRAFT` | `ISSUED` | `PARTIALLY_PAID` | `PAID` | `CANCELLED`),
   - `pdfUrl` (nullable cached URL), `createdByUserId` (FK), `createdAt`, `updatedAt`
7. **`invoice_line_items`**:
   - `id`, `invoiceId` (FK cascade delete), `sortOrder` (int), `description` (text), `sacCode` (varchar), `amountUsd` (numeric 12,2, nullable for descriptive sub-lines where the grouped total sits on the main/summary line, OR itemized amounts per line)
8. **`remittance_reconciliations`** (USD -> INR Settlement Tracking):
   - `id`, `invoiceId` (FK), `receivedDate` (date), `invoicedUsd` (numeric 12,2), `receivedUsd` (numeric 12,2 — after any foreign bank deduction), `bankChargesUsd` (numeric 12,2 — auto-calculated `invoicedUsd - receivedUsd`), `exchangeRateInr` (numeric 10,4 — e.g., `84.3500`), `grossInrAmount` (numeric 14,2), `bankInrDeductions` (numeric 12,2, default 0), `netInrCredited` (numeric 14,2), `fircNumber` (varchar, nullable), `bankReferenceNumber` (varchar), `notes` (text), `reconciledByUserId` (FK), `createdAt`

### 4. EXACT 2-PAGE PDF LAYOUT REQUIREMENTS
Replicate the statutory Indian GST Export Invoice structure cleanly:

**PAGE 1: TAX INVOICE**
- **Header Banner:** "INVOICE" / "TAX INVOICE" + LUT Export Clause: *"Supply Meant For Export Under Bond Or Letter of Undertaking Without Payment Of Integrated Goods and Service Tax (IGST)"*
- **Top Two-Column Grid:**
  - **Left Box (Supplier / Freelancer):** Full Name, Address, PIN, `GSTIN: <gstin>`
  - **Right Box (Invoice Metadata):** `Serial No. of Invoice`, `Invoice Date`, `Place of Supply: Outside India`, `Country of Supply: <Customer Country>`, `Amount of Tax Subject to Reverse Charges: No / Nil`
- **Receiver Box (Details of Receiver - Billed To):**
  - `Name`, `Address`, `State / Province`, `State Code`, `Country`, `GSTIN / Unique ID`
- **Line Items & Tax Table:**
  - Columns: `S.No` | `Description of Services` | `SAC Code` | `Total` | `Taxable Value` | `CGST (Rate / Amt)` | `SGST (Rate / Amt)` | `IGST (Rate / Amt)`
  - Support both multi-line descriptions rolled into one primary billed row (matching the sample where multiple work lines share S.No 1 and `USD 1,750`) AND individually priced rows.
  - Totals Row (`Total Taxable Value`, `0.00` taxes) + Grand Total Row (`Invoice Total: USD X,XXX.XX`) + Amount in Words (e.g., *"USD One Thousand Seven Hundred Fifty Only"*).
- **Footer Compliance Grid:**
  - **Left Column:** Certification statement (*"Certified that the particulars given above are true and correct and the amount indicated represents the price actually charged..."*) + `TERMS OF SALE` (Jurisdiction clause + custom terms).
  - **Right Column:** `For <Freelancer Full Name>` + `GSTIN: <gstin>` + Signature image (if uploaded) + `Authorised Signatory`.

**PAGE 2: WIRE TRANSFER / TELEGRAPHIC TRANSFER INSTRUCTIONS (USD)**
- If `useUploadedDocForPage2` is true and a PDF is uploaded, merge that uploaded PDF as Page 2 using `pdf-lib`.
- Otherwise, render a crisp, structured Page 2 titled **"INSTRUCTIONS FOR WIRE TRANSFER / TELEGRAPHIC TRANSFER OF PAYMENTS (USD)"** with note *"(Bank charges for processing wire transfer to be paid by the remitter)"* containing 3 clean bordered sections:
  1. **Intermediary / Correspondent / Nostro Bank** (Bank Name, SWIFT Code, CHIPS UID, FED ABA Routing Number, Nostro Account Number)
  2. **Beneficiary Bank (Receiving Bank in India)** (Bank Name, Branch Address, SWIFT Code)
  3. **Final Beneficiary Details (Recipient)** (Account Holder Name, Account Number, Account Holder Address, **Purpose of Remittance Code & Description**)

### 5. APPLICATION PAGES & UI MODULES

   - **KPI Cards:** Total Billed (USD), Total Realized (USD), Total INR Credited, Pending Reconciliation (USD), Average Realized Exchange Rate (INR/USD).
   - **Consolidated Monthly Breakdown Table:** Shows every freelancer's billing status for the selected month (Billed / Not Billed yet, Invoice #, Customer, Billed USD, Received USD, Exchange Rate, Net INR Credited, Status badge, Quick Actions: Download 2-Page PDF, Record Payment).
   - **Individual Freelancer View:** Drill-down view per freelancer showing their multi-month billing history, cumulative annual GST export turnover (crucial in India for tracking ₹20L GST threshold / LUT compliance), and pending payments.
3. **`/invoices/generate` (End-of-Month Batch Billing Wizard — Primary Workflow):**
   - **Step 1:** Choose Billing Month & Year, Invoice Date, and Customer Company.

4. **`/invoices` & `/invoices/[id]` (Invoice Ledger & Reconciliation):**
   - Filterable/searchable invoice registry.
   - Detail view showing the embedded 2-page PDF preview, download button, edit draft capability, and **"Record Inward Remittance (USD -> INR)"** modal/drawer.
   - Reconciliation calculator auto-computes `Bank Wire Fee (USD) = Invoiced USD - Received USD` and `Gross INR = Received USD × Exchange Rate`, allowing entry of any local bank INR deduction and FIRC number.
5. **`/freelancers` & `/freelancers/[id]` (Freelancer & Bank Profile Master):**
   - Manage freelancer GSTIN, address, LUT details, invoice numbering format, signature upload, and **Bank Information / Page 2 Upload Section** (structured wire transfer form + PDF/image upload for Page 2).
6. **`/customers` (Client Companies Master):**
   - Manage foreign client companies, addresses, country of supply, tax IDs.
7. **`/services` (Work Description Master):**
   - Manage reusable service description lines (e.g., QA consultancy, React development, configuration) with default SAC codes.
8. **`/users` (Admin & User Access Management):**
   - Create/deactivate Admin or Freelancer user accounts and link Freelancer users to their respective freelancer profile.
